Descriptions

Short

PuzzleBee is a home for your jigsaw puzzles: scan a box to add it, time every solve, and see what your friends finish.

Medium

PuzzleBee puts your jigsaw shelf, solve timer and puzzling friends in one calm app. Scan a box to add it, time every session, and find your next favorite among nearly 10,000 puzzles. It's free, with no ads and no tracking.

Long

PuzzleBee is a home for your jigsaw puzzles: the ones on your shelf, the one on your table right now, and the ones you're dreaming about.

Keep your shelf. Scan a box's barcode to add it in a tap, or search nearly 10,000 puzzles. Sort what you own, what you're solving and what you want, and add a puzzle that isn't in the catalog yet with a photo of the box.

Time every solve. A simple timer pauses and resumes across sessions. See how your time compares with everyone else who finished it, with every solve kept, repeat solves included.

Find your next favorite. Charts of what puzzlers are adding and finishing this month, a daily pick, new arrivals, themes, and recommendations based on the puzzles already on your shelf.

Ratings from real puzzlers. Star ratings and reviews from people who finished the puzzle, with spoiler warnings so nobody gives away the final picture.

Puzzle with friends. The Hive shows what your friends finish, quietly and in order. Cheer them on with a like or a comment, and add friends by username, QR code or link.

Watch your year add up. Streaks, pieces placed and hours puzzled, badges and titles you earn as you go, and a weekly, monthly or yearly goal if you like one.

Calm by design: no ads, no tracking, and no marketing notifications. Reminders are optional, and set by you.
