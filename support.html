Support

We aim to reply within two business days.

The quickest way to reach us is from the app: You → Settings → Help & feedback. Your message includes your app version, which helps us fix things faster. You can also email support@puzzlebee.app.

Common questions

How do I sign in?

Use Sign in with Apple, Google, or your email address. With email, we send you a 6-digit code. There's no password to remember.

A barcode didn't find my puzzle.

Add it from the scan screen with a photo of the box. If it's private, only you will see it. You can also submit it, and once we've checked it, it joins the catalog for everyone.

Something about a puzzle is wrong.

At the bottom of the puzzle's page, tap "Report an issue or suggest an edit".

How do I report or block someone?

To report a review or comment, tap its ••• menu and choose Report. To block someone, open their profile, tap ••• and choose Block: you won't see their activity, reviews or comments, and they can't add you. We review every report within 24 hours. You can unblock people in Settings → Blocked people.

How do reminders work?

Turn them on in Settings → Reminders and pick a time. They're scheduled on your phone, and PuzzleBee never sends marketing notifications.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to You → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, shelf, solve history, reviews and friends. If you signed in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm, and we remove PuzzleBee from your Apple ID. Can't get into the app? Email support@puzzlebee.app from the address on your account and we'll do it for you.

Where does my data go?

See our Privacy Policy. In short: no ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.
