Features

Built for the way puzzlers actually puzzle.

Six things PuzzleBee does well, from the moment you pick up a box to the moment the last piece goes in.

Keep your shelf

Your shelf, kept tidy.

Every puzzle you own, the one on your table right now, and the ones you're dreaming about, in one place.

  • Scan a box's barcode to add it in a tap, or search nearly 10,000 puzzles
  • Sort what you own, what you're solving and what you want
  • Add a puzzle we don't have yet with a photo of the box
Time every solve

A timer that waits for you.

Real puzzles take more than one sitting. Start the timer when you sit down, pause it when you get up, and pick up where you left off tomorrow.

  • A simple timer that pauses and resumes across sessions
  • See how your time compares with everyone else who finished it
  • Your solve history kept for every puzzle, including repeat solves
Discover

Find your next favorite.

Never run out of ideas for the next box. Discover always has somewhere else to go.

  • Charts of what puzzlers are adding and finishing this month
  • A daily pick, new arrivals and themes to browse
  • Recommendations based on the puzzles already on your shelf
Ratings and reviews

Ratings from real puzzlers.

Know about the puzzle dust and the satisfying cut before you buy, from people who actually finished it.

  • Star ratings and reviews from people who finished the puzzle
  • Spoiler warnings, so nobody gives away the final picture
  • Mark the reviews that helped you
The Hive

Puzzle with friends.

A feed of what your friends finish, quietly and in order. No algorithm, no strangers, no noise.

  • See what your friends finish, and how long it took them
  • Cheer them on with a like or a comment
  • Add friends by username, QR code or link
Your year

Watch your year add up.

Every finish counts toward something, without turning your hobby into a chore.

  • Streaks, pieces placed and hours puzzled
  • Badges and titles you earn as you go
  • A weekly, monthly or yearly goal, if you like one
And the small things

Details that make it feel right

iPhone and iPad

Every orientation and Split View on iPad, so it can sit beside the puzzle table.

Dark mode

A warm dark theme for one more session after everyone's gone to bed.

Gentle reminders

Optional daily reminders, set by you and scheduled on your device. Nothing else.

Sign in your way

Sign in with Apple, Google or your email. Delete your account and data from Settings at any time.

Where to buy

Puzzle pages link to stores that carry them, so your wishlist is one tap from your table.

We read every message

Tap Help & feedback in Settings and it comes straight to us. Support
