Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

PuzzleBee ("we", "us", "our") is an app for keeping track of the jigsaw puzzles you own, solve and want, and sharing them with friends. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. The short version: we collect what the app needs to work, we don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

Things you give us

  • Account: your email address, and your name if you sign in with Apple or Google and choose to share it. If you use Sign in with Apple's "Hide My Email", we only see the relay address.
  • Profile: your username, display name, and optionally a profile picture, a short bio and a puzzling goal.
  • Your shelf: the puzzles you add, whether they're on your shelf, in progress, finished or on your wishlist, your solve sessions and times, and anyone you credit as a solving partner.
  • Things you post: star ratings, written reviews, comments and likes on friends' activity, and "helpful" votes on reviews.
  • Puzzles you add to the catalog: details and box photos for a puzzle we don't have yet.
  • Friends: friend requests you send and accept, and people you block.
  • Reports and support: reports you make about a review, comment or puzzle listing, and messages you send through Help & feedback. Support messages include the app version and your device's operating system version, so we can reproduce problems.

Things the app records as you use it

  • Activity: events such as finishing a puzzle, earning a badge or sharing a puzzle, which power your stats, streaks, badges and your friends' Hive feed.
  • Store link taps: when you tap a "Where to buy" link, we record which puzzle and store it was, and your account, so we know which links people find useful. Some of these links are affiliate links (see below).

Things we don't collect

  • We don't use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we don't use advertising identifiers.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't collect your location or your contacts.
  • Camera images used for barcode scanning are processed on your device. A photo is only uploaded if you choose to attach it to a puzzle or use it as your profile picture.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your device. We don't send push notifications.

How we use it

  • To run the app: your shelf, timer, stats, badges, recommendations, friends and Hive.
  • To keep the community safe: we review reports, and posts such as reviews may be screened by automated tools that we run ourselves, before a person decides whether to remove anything.
  • To build the puzzle catalog from submissions.
  • To answer support requests and fix bugs.
  • To send the emails sign-in needs, such as one-time sign-in codes. We don't send marketing email.

Who can see what

  • Everyone using PuzzleBee can see your username, display name, profile picture, bio, and your ratings and reviews.
  • Your friends can see your activity in their Hive feed (puzzles you finish or share, badges you earn), and the comments and likes on it.
  • Only you can see your shelf, wishlist and solve sessions. Puzzles you add privately stay private.
  • People you block no longer see your activity, and you no longer see theirs.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers that run parts of PuzzleBee for us, under contracts that limit what they can do with it:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and file storage (privacy policy).
  • Apple and Google: only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Expo: delivers app updates. Your device contacts Expo's servers to check for updates.
  • UPCitemdb: when you scan a barcode we don't recognize, the barcode number is looked up there. Nothing about you is sent with it.

When you tap a store link, you leave PuzzleBee and that store's own privacy policy applies. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases, and other store links may also earn us a commission. This never changes the price you pay, and stores don't receive your PuzzleBee account information from us.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, shelf, solve history, reviews, comments, likes, friends, badges and profile pictures straight away, and photos attached to puzzles you kept private. Puzzle details and photos you submitted that are now part of the public catalog stay in the catalog, without your name. Store link tap records are kept without your account. Deleted data may stay in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before it is overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app: You → Settings → Delete account. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke PuzzleBee's access to your Apple ID.
  • Correct your profile in Settings → Edit profile, and edit or delete your reviews at any time.
  • Get a copy of your data, or ask us anything about it, by emailing support@puzzlebee.app. We'll reply within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or California), you may have more rights, such as objecting to processing or complaining to your data protection authority. We honor them for everyone, wherever you live. We process your information because it's needed to provide the app you signed up for, and to keep the community safe.

Security

Your data is encrypted in transit and stored with Supabase, with database rules that only let each person read their own private data. Sign-in tokens are kept in your device's secure keychain.

Children

PuzzleBee isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before the change takes effect, and update the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@puzzlebee.app.
