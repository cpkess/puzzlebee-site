About

Made by a puzzle collector, for puzzlers.

"I kept a spreadsheet to track my puzzles. Then I lost the spreadsheet. I built PuzzleBee so I'd never need a spreadsheet again."

PuzzleBee started with a problem every puzzle collector knows: you buy a puzzle, solve it, and a year later you can't remember if you own it, what you thought of it, or how long it took. You buy the same puzzle twice. You lose track of your wishlist. You forget which ones are still on the shelf.

A spreadsheet sort of works, until it doesn't. It can't scan a barcode. It can't time a solve that takes five evenings. It can't tell you which puzzles other people loved, or share your finish with a friend who puzzles too.

So we built PuzzleBee: a real app, made for the way people actually puzzle.

What makes it different

Most tracking apps are a database with a search box. PuzzleBee is a companion for the whole hobby, from the moment you pick up the box to the moment the last piece goes in.

The timer understands that real solves happen across many sittings. The scanner means you never type a piece count again. Reviews come from people who finished the puzzle. And the Hive lets you share with friends without the noise of social media.

It's also calm on purpose. There are no ads, no tracking and no marketing notifications. PuzzleBee earns a small commission when you buy a puzzle through some of its store links, and that's it.

The puzzle catalog

PuzzleBee launches with nearly 10,000 jigsaw puzzles, each with its maker, piece count and picture, and the catalog grows every week. If you own a puzzle we don't have yet, add it with a photo of the box. We check every one before it appears for everyone else.
